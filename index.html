01 — Profile

  • Role Senior backend engineer
  • Sector Investment banking
  • Availability 99.9% target
  • Based Mumbai, IN

I solve scaling and reliability problems in the financial sector. The work is high-concurrency distributed systems, resilient microservices and event-driven architecture — designed so that the interesting failures are boring by the time they reach production.

Seven years across Capgemini, BNP Paribas, Barclays and now JPMorgan Chase have taught me that most outages are not clever. They are an expired credential, a queue nobody watched, a retry storm with no backoff. So I spend my time on the unglamorous half of system design: what happens when the dependency is slow, who gets told, and how the thing recovers without a human.

02 — Operating principle The Shawshank Redemption

Entropy is the default state of every system I have ever owned. Certificates expire, queues back up, dashboards quietly stop reporting. Engineering is the refusal.

Aug 2022 — Jul 2026

Software Engineer BA4 — platform modernization & resilience

Legacy → high availability

Problem: legacy CLI tools bottlenecked scheduling and management of global Autosys jobs, with no robust authorization and no room to scale.

Built: a high-availability Spring Boot microservices ecosystem behind a global REST API, with a custom multi-constraint authorization layer over a Redis cache-aside pattern and circuit breakers for fault tolerance under load.

Low-latency event routing

Problem: alert deduplication and routing inefficiencies at the source were delaying enterprise security visibility.

Built: an end-to-end alert enrichment and preprocessing engine in Python and Nginx, with dynamic schema-driven routing that redirects alerts instantly and measurably sharpened monitoring accuracy.
